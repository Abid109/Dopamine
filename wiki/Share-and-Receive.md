# Share and Receive

Send your data to another phone and let the other person choose what to keep. Open **Share & Receive** in the sidebar.

## Sending

1. Tick what to include: Profile & goals, App settings, Workout logs, Calendar plans, Notes, Workout time totals.
2. Choose how to send:
   - **Show QR code:** the app shows a loop of QR codes.
   - **Send as file:** the Android share sheet opens so you can use Bluetooth, Quick Share, WhatsApp or anything installed.

For QR, the data is compressed and split into numbered codes, shown one after another. Use Previous, Pause and Next if the other phone needs more time. Small data fits in one or two codes. Large data needs more, and the app suggests sending a file when there are many.

## Receiving

- **Scan QR code:** point the camera at the other phone. A progress bar shows how many codes you have. The order does not matter and repeats are fine. When all are collected, the data is saved automatically.
- **Import from file:** pick a file that was sent with **Send as file**. A full JSON backup also works.

## The Received files list

Every received transfer is kept in **Received files**, showing who sent it, when, and what it contains. It is marked **New** until you import from it.

Nothing on your phone changes until you open **Review & import**.

## Choosing what to import

The review screen lists the items inside the file. Tick the ones you want:

| Item | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Profile | Replaces your profile details with the received ones |
| Settings | Replaces theme, colours and timer settings |
| Workout logs | Listed by day. Only entries you do not already have are added |
| Calendar plans | Listed by day. Only missing plans are added |
| Notes | New notes are added. If a note exists but differs, it is marked **changed** and replaces yours only if you tick it |
| Workout time | Keeps the larger time for each day |

Items you already have are greyed out and labelled **already have**. Use **All** or **None** on a section to select quickly. Tap **Import selected** to apply.

Importing the same file twice never creates duplicates.

## Deleting

Tap **Delete** on a received file to remove it. Data you already imported stays on your phone.

## Tips

- Both phones need a steady hold and good light. Turn screen brightness up on the sending phone.
- The first scan asks for camera permission. Allow it.
- Wi-Fi or Bluetooth is not needed for QR. For Bluetooth or Quick Share, use **Send as file**.
