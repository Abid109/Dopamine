# Dopamine

Dopamine is a calisthenics workout tracker for Android. Log your sets, plan routines, track runs and walks with GPS, follow your streak and share progress between phones. Everything is saved privately on your own phone.

**[Download the APK](https://github.com/Abid109/Dopamine/releases/download/latest/Dopamine.apk)**

## At a glance

- 18 built-in calisthenics movements plus custom exercises
- Calorie estimates based on your bodyweight
- Daily streak, goals and a weekly burn chart
- **Run and walk tracking** with GPS that keeps recording with the screen off, a live map, goals and a route history
- Calendar with routine templates and full-screen reminders
- Rest timer and session stopwatch
- Notifications with sound, vibration and the Dopamine icon
- Optional email sign in with cloud backup and sync
- **In-app updates** that install over the old app and keep your data
- QR and file sharing with selective import
- PDF fitness report and JSON backup
- 15 themes (including 8 soft serif themes) and 6 accent colours
- 7 navigation styles, a floating-card Settings menu and a Smooth mode for older phones
