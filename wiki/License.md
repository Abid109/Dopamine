## The two options

1. **AGPL-3.0 (free).** The standard open-source option. The main rule is "share alike": if you share the app or a changed version, or let others use a changed version over a network, you share its source under the AGPL-3.0 as well.
2. **Commercial license (by written agreement).** For anyone who wants to use the code without the AGPL conditions. It exists only if the copyright holder agrees in writing. To ask, open an issue titled **Commercial license request** on the [issues page](https://github.com/Abid109/Dopamine/issues).
