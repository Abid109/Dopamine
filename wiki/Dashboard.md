# Dashboard

The Dashboard is your daily overview. It updates as soon as you log an exercise or save a timer session.

## Today cards

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Today's Sets** | Number of movements logged today, against your daily movement goal. |
| **Burn Today** | Calories estimated for today and the percentage of your goal. |
| **Streak** | Consecutive active days. |
| **Active Time** | Time saved from the session stopwatch today. |
| **Workouts** | Total number of active days. |
| **Lifetime Burn** | All calories logged since you started. |

## Daily targets

- **Daily Calorie Target** shows progress and calories remaining.
- **Daily Exercises Target** shows how many more movements reach your goal.

Change both goals in [Profile and Biometrics](Profile-and-Biometrics).

## Weekly chart

**Weekly Caloric Burn (Mon to Sun)** shows one point per day. Tap a point to see that day's calories and number of movements.

## Streak rules

A day counts as active if you logged at least one exercise or saved more than one minute on the session timer. The streak continues if you were active yesterday, even before you train today.

## Today's logged exercises

The list at the bottom shows what you logged today. Use **Add More** to jump to the logger.
