# Sign In and Sync

Logging in is **optional**. Without an account, the app works exactly as it always has and everything stays on your phone. With an account, your data is backed up and kept in sync across all your devices.

Open **Profile**. The sign in card is at the top. A green dot appears next to **Profile** in the menu while you are signed in. After you sign in, the sign in form is replaced by your account details and a **Sign out** button.

## Create account

1. Tap **Create account**.
2. Enter your email and a password of at least 8 characters, twice.
3. Tap **Create account**.
4. If the app asks for a code, check your email, type the code, and tap **Confirm**. If the email has a link instead, tap the link, then come back and sign in.

## Sign in

Enter your email and password and tap **Sign in**. Data already on the phone is kept and merged with your cloud backup.

## What is synced

Profile, settings, workout logs, calendar plans, notes and workout time.

Not synced: files received through [Share and Receive](Share-and-Receive), which stay on each phone, and a very large profile photo, which stays on the phone it was added on.

## How syncing works

- It runs when you open the app, a few seconds after you change something, when you return to the app, and when your phone comes back online.
- Tap **Sync now** to sync immediately. The page shows the last sync time.
- Two phones are **merged**, not overwritten. If you log workouts on two phones before syncing, you get both. Deleting something on one phone removes it on the others.
- If you change the same note on two phones, the most recent edit wins.
- Without internet, the app keeps working and syncs later.

## Forgot password

1. Tap **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email and tap **Send code**.
3. Type the code from the email and choose a new password.

## Sign out

Tap **Sign out**. Your data stays on the phone, and syncing stops. If you sign in later with a different account, the app asks whether to add the phone's data to that account or erase it first.

## Delete your cloud backup

Tap **Delete my cloud backup**. Data on your phones is not touched. If you stay signed in, the next sync uploads a fresh copy, so sign out first if you want syncing to stop.

## Moving to a new phone

Install the app, open **Profile**, and sign in. Your data downloads automatically.

## Privacy

See [Privacy and Data](Privacy-and-Data). Each user can only access their own backup.

## The sign in screen

The sign in card has a welcome banner and two tabs, **Sign in** and **Create account**. Each field has an icon, and the eye button shows or hides your password. While you create an account, a strength bar shows how strong your password is. The email code is typed into one large field. Pressing Enter on the keyboard submits the form.
