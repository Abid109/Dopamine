# Privacy and Data

- All your data is stored **on your phone** in the app's storage.
- Accounts are **optional**. If you never log in, nothing is sent to any server.
- There is no tracking or advertising.
- Data only leaves your phone when you share it ([Share and Receive](Share-and-Receive)), export a backup, create a PDF, or turn on cloud backup by logging in ([Sign In and Sync](Account-and-Sync)).
- The camera is used only on the QR scanning screen and nothing is recorded.
- **Location** is used only while you record a run or walk. Routes are saved on the phone only and are never synced, shared or uploaded.
- Fonts, styling and the PDF library are bundled inside the app. The app contacts public networks only to show map pictures from OpenStreetMap, to look for app updates on GitHub, and for the optional sign in and sync. No personal data is included in those requests.

Read the full [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/Abid109/Dopamine/blob/main/PRIVACY.md). The same explanation is inside the app under **Settings**, **Privacy**.

## What the map and updates reveal

- **Map:** the map pictures (tiles) come from OpenStreetMap. Those requests reveal the map area you are viewing to that service, not your name or account.
- **Updates:** the app asks GitHub for the newest release now and then. GitHub sees your IP address like any website visit. The APK is downloaded only when you tap Update.

## Where is my data?

Inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes it. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

## Deleting data

- Remove your cloud backup: **Profile**, **Delete my cloud backup**.
- Remove one received file: **Share & Receive**, **Delete**.
- Remove a run or walk: open the **Run** tab and tap **Delete** next to it in the history.
- Erase everything (including runs and walks): **Settings**, **Backup & data**, **Reset All Data**.
