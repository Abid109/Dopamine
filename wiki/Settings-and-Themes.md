Open **Settings** from the menu.

### Soft & serif themes

Warm pastel backgrounds with cream cards, rounded corners and a serif typeface. On the Dashboard they also show an illustration of athletes across the top.

| Theme | Style |
| --- | --- |
| Sunrise | Warm Apricot |
| Blush | Rose Petal |
| Mint | Garden Mint |
| Sky | Clear Morning |
| Lilac | Soft Lavender |

The serif font loads from the internet. Without a connection the app uses the phone's own serif font.

## Accent shade

Amber Gold, Sage Emerald, Electric Slate, Power Crimson, Royal Violet and Flame Coral.

In the soft themes, **Amber Gold** takes on the theme's own colour (apricot for Sunrise, rose for Blush, green for Mint, blue for Sky, purple for Lilac). Pick any other accent to override it.

## Font scale

- **Compact:** dense data
- **Comfortable:** standard readability

## Timer and clock

- **Clock Display Format:** switch between 12-hour AM/PM and 24-hour.
- **Default Rest Timer Duration:** the starting time of the [rest timer](Timers).
