# Run and Walk Tracking

The **Run** tab records a walk or run with your phone's GPS. It keeps recording when the screen is off or you switch to another app.

## First time

Press **Start**. Dopamine asks for:

1. **Location** (required). Without it nothing can be recorded.
2. **Notifications.** Android shows a small "Dopamine is tracking" notification while GPS is on, and goal alerts use it too.
3. **Battery optimization.** Allow it so Android does not stop tracking after a while. On some phones (Xiaomi, Huawei, Samsung) also open **App info** and set Battery to Unrestricted.

You can check or change all three in **Settings, Timers & Alerts**.

## Recording

- Pick **Walk** or **Run**, choose a goal, press **Start**.
- **Pause** stops the clock and distance (for a traffic light or a break). **Resume** continues and does not count the distance travelled while paused.
- **Finish** saves the activity to your history. Tap an entry to see its route on the map.
- If the app is closed while you are recording, the route so far is kept and you can resume or save it next time.

## Goals

- **Distance** (km) or **Time** (minutes): a progress bar fills and you get a notification when you reach it.
- **Map pin:** tap the map to place a flag. The bar shows how far you have left.
- **Free:** no goal.

## Accuracy

Weak or jumpy GPS readings (worse than about 35 m), tiny jitter while standing still, and impossible jumps are ignored. The screen shows the current GPS accuracy. For best results start in the open and wait for the accuracy to drop below 20 m.

## Calories

Estimated from distance, time and the weight in your Profile with the standard walking and running equations. It follows your **Calorie Count** setting (active only or including resting).

## Privacy

Routes are stored on the phone only and are not synced. See [Privacy and Data](Privacy-and-Data).
