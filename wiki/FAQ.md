# FAQ

**Is it free?**
Yes. Dopamine is open source under the MIT licence.

**Do I need an account?**
No. Logging in is optional and only adds cloud backup and sync. See [Sign In and Sync](Account-and-Sync).

**Does it work offline?**
Mostly. Logging, planning, notes and QR sharing work offline after the first launch. Styling and fonts load from the internet at launch, and the PDF library loads the first time you make a PDF.

**Is there an iPhone version?**
Not at the moment. Releases are Android APKs.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your phone only. See [Privacy and Data](Privacy-and-Data).

**How accurate are the calories?**
They are estimates based on intensity, time, reps and your weight. See [Calorie Calculation](Calorie-Calculation).

**How do I move to a new phone?**
The easiest way is to sign in on the new phone with the same email ([Sign In and Sync](Account-and-Sync)). You can also use [Share and Receive](Share-and-Receive) with QR codes or a file, or **Export JSON** and **Import Backup**.

**Can I share only some of my data?**
Yes. Tick only what you want before sending, and the receiving phone then picks exactly what to import.

**Will receiving data overwrite mine?**
Only the items you tick. Logs and plans are added without duplicates. Notes and profile are replaced only if you choose.

**How do I get updates?**
Open **Settings**, **App updates** and tap **Download and install**. The app also shows a prompt when a new version is found. You can still download the newest APK from the [Releases page](https://github.com/Abid109/Dopamine/releases/tag/latest) and install it over the old app. Your data stays either way. See [Updating the App](App-Updates).

**Why does Android still ask me to confirm an update?**
Android always shows its own confirmation when an app installs another app or an update. No app can skip it. Tap **Update** and the new version replaces the old one.

**Does run and walk tracking work with the screen off?**
Yes. It uses a foreground service, so GPS keeps recording while the screen is off or another app is open. Allow location, notifications and battery optimization for the best results. See [Run and Walk Tracking](Run-and-Walk-Tracking).

**Why does it ask for battery optimization?**
Android can stop apps that run for a long time to save power. Allowing Dopamine to ignore battery optimization stops that from cutting a long run short. See [Notifications and Permissions](Notifications-and-Permissions).

**Do my runs and walks sync to the cloud?**
No. Routes are stored on your phone only. They are not part of sign in and sync, Share and Receive or reports.

**Does the map work offline?**
The route is still recorded without internet, but the map pictures need a connection. Without it you see your route line on a blank background.

**Are the notifications silent?**
No. They arrive as normal alerts with sound, vibration and the Dopamine icon. You can change the sound per notification type in Android settings. See [Notifications and Permissions](Notifications-and-Permissions).

**Can I use miles?**
Not yet. Distances and pace are in kilometres.

**Can I contribute?**
Yes. See [Contributing](Contributing).

**Where can I read about every change?**
[What's New](Whats-New) summarises recent changes, and the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/Abid109/Dopamine/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) lists all of them.
