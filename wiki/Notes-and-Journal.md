# Notes and Journal

Keep training cues, routine ideas and reflections in one place.

## Create a note

1. Open **Notes & Journal**.
2. Tap **New Note**.
3. Choose a **Category Tag**: Workout Plan, Daily Plan, Journal or Other.
4. Add a **Title** and your **Content**.
5. Tap **Save Note**.

## Find notes

Use the tag filter at the top (All, Workout Plan, Daily Plan, Journal, Other) to show one type of note.

## Edit and delete

Tap a note to edit it. You can delete notes you no longer need.

## Sharing notes

Notes can be sent to another phone with [Share and Receive](Share-and-Receive). If the other phone already has a note with changes, it is marked as changed and only replaced if you choose.
