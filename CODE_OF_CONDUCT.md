# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Dopamine to be a friendly, respectful project for everyone, whatever their age, background, experience or fitness level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give feedback that is specific and respectful.
- Accept that people have different views and skill levels.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, or trolling in issues and pull requests.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, open an issue titled "Conduct" without sharing private details, and a maintainer will arrange a private conversation.
