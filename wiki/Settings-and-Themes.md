# Settings and Themes

Open **Settings** from the navigation. It is a menu of rounded cards: each row has an icon, a name and an arrow. Tap a row to open that page, and tap the back arrow (or use your phone's back button) to return to the menu.

| Card | Row | What you find there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preferences | **Appearance & style** | Colour theme, accent shade, font scale, navigation style and performance |
| Preferences | **Timers & alerts** | Calorie count, clock format, default rest timer, notifications, full-screen reminders and the permissions for run and walk tracking |
| Preferences | **Run & walk tracking** | Opens the Run tab ([Run and Walk Tracking](Run-and-Walk-Tracking)) |
| Data & privacy | **Backup & data** | JSON backup, import, PDF report and reset ([PDF Reports and Backup](PDF-Reports-and-Backup)) |
| Data & privacy | **Privacy** | What is stored, what leaves the phone, permissions and how to delete data ([Privacy and Data](Privacy-and-Data)) |
| About | **App updates** | Installed and newest version, and installing an update ([Updating the App](App-Updates)) |

The sections below describe the pages in this order.

#### Soft & serif themes

Warm pastel backgrounds with cream cards, rounded corners and a serif typeface. On the Dashboard the first five show an illustration of athletes across the top. **Dream**, **Aim High** and **No Risk** are inspired by motivational posters: each shows a poster picture and caption in a banner at the top of the Dashboard (a chess pawn for Dream, a snowy summit for Aim High, a climber on a rope for No Risk).

| Theme | Style |
| --- | --- |
| Sunrise | Warm Apricot |
| Blush | Rose Petal |
| Mint | Garden Mint |
| Sky | Clear Morning |
| Lilac | Soft Lavender |
| Dream | Impossible Dream: deep navy and cream |
| Aim High | Summit Red: bold red |
| No Risk | Open Sky: bright blue and green |

The serif font loads from the internet. Without a connection the app uses the phone's own serif font.

### Accent shade

Amber Gold, Sage Emerald, Electric Slate, Power Crimson, Royal Violet and Flame Coral.

In the soft themes, **Amber Gold** takes on the theme's own colour (apricot for Sunrise, rose for Blush, green for Mint, blue for Sky, purple for Lilac). Pick any other accent to override it.

### Font scale

- **Compact:** dense data
- **Comfortable:** standard readability

### Navigation style

Choose how you move around the app. The change applies at once and is remembered.

| Style | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Sidebar | The classic side menu opened with the menu button |
| Bottom bar | Tabs along the bottom of the screen |
| Floating dock | A rounded bar floating above the bottom. The active tab shows its name |
| Center button | A bottom bar with a raised **Log** button in the middle and a **More** tab for Share and Settings |
| Top tabs | A scrollable strip of tabs under the header |
| Side rail | A slim column of icons on the left |
| Floating menu | One round button in the corner that opens a menu |

In every style your Profile is one tap away on the avatar in the header. The Android back button closes an open menu first.

### Performance

**Smooth mode** removes shadows, blur and animations so the app runs better on older phones. Nothing else changes.

- **Auto** (default): turns Smooth mode on for slower phones.
- **Smooth mode**: always on.
- **Full effects**: always off.

## Timers & alerts

### Calorie count

- **Active calories** (default): counts only the energy the exercise adds on top of resting.
- **Include resting**: also counts the energy you burn at rest during the exercise.

Workouts you already logged keep their saved numbers. See [Calorie Calculation](Calorie-Calculation).

### Timer and clock

- **Clock Display Format:** switch between 12-hour AM/PM and 24-hour.
- **Default Rest Timer Duration:** the starting time of the [rest timer](Timers).

### Notifications

Turn notifications on or off, and allow full-screen reminders. See [Notifications and Permissions](Notifications-and-Permissions).

### Run & walk tracking permissions

Shows whether Notifications, Location and Battery optimization are allowed, with a button to allow each one. See [Run and Walk Tracking](Run-and-Walk-Tracking).
