# What's New

The newest changes first. The complete list with every detail is the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/Abid109/Dopamine/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) in the repository.

## Latest

- **Run and walk tracking.** A new Run tab records a walk or run with GPS, even with the screen off. It shows a live map, time, distance, pace and calories, supports pause and resume, and keeps a history of your routes. See [Run and Walk Tracking](Run-and-Walk-Tracking).
- **Goals.** Aim for a distance, a time, or a flag you place on the map, and get a notification when you reach it.
- **In-app updates.** **Settings**, **App updates** finds new versions and installs them without leaving the app. See [Updating the App](App-Updates).
- **Notifications are no longer silent.** Alerts now come with sound, vibration and a pop-up, and show the Dopamine icon. See [Notifications and Permissions](Notifications-and-Permissions).
- **A new Settings menu.** Settings is a list of rounded floating cards, and each card opens its own page. See [Settings and Themes](Settings-and-Themes).
- **Permissions in one place.** **Settings**, **Timers & alerts** shows Notifications, Location and Battery optimization with an Allow button for each.
- **Full-screen reminders.** Calendar reminders can open a full screen over the lock screen.
- **Poster themes.** Dream, Aim High and No Risk join the soft serif themes.
- The floating dock is the default navigation for new installs.

## Earlier

- **1.8.0:** a more accurate calorie estimate and the Calorie Count setting. See [Calorie Calculation](Calorie-Calculation).
- **1.7.0:** seven navigation styles, Smooth mode and a redesigned sign in.
- **1.6.0:** five soft serif themes.

Older versions are listed in the CHANGELOG.
