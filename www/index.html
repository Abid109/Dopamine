Local Data & Export

Privacy details

Tap a topic to see exactly what the app does with your data. Dopamine keeps your fitness data on your phone.

What the app stores

Your profile, workout logs, plans, notes, timers and settings are saved in the app storage on your phone, including an optional profile photo.

What the app does not do

No analytics, advertising or tracking. No account is required. The developer does not read your data.

When data leaves your phone

Sign in and sync (optional): if you sign in with an email on the Profile page, a copy of your profile, settings, workout logs, plans, notes and workout time is stored in a private cloud database (Supabase) so your devices stay in sync. Each user can only read and change their own row. A very large profile photo stays on the device. Signing out stops syncing.

Share & Receive: you choose what to include and how to send it. PDF report and JSON backup: created on your phone and shared only by you.

Permissions

Camera: only on the QR scanning screen while it is open. Images are never saved or sent.

Notifications and full-screen reminders: used only to show your timers, run and walk tracking, and the reminders you set in Calendar and Planning. They are created on your phone.

App updates: the app asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the newest release now and then, and downloads the new APK from github.com only when you tap Update. Nothing about you or your data is sent. Android asks you to confirm every install.

Location and battery: used only while you record a run or walk, so GPS keeps working with the screen off. Routes are saved on this phone only and are not synced or uploaded. Deleting an activity or using Reset All Data removes them.

Internet: used for page styling, fonts, the PDF library, optional sign in and sync, and map pictures from OpenStreetMap (the tiles requested reveal the area you view, not your name or account).

Deleting your data

Delete your cloud backup from Profile, then Delete my cloud backup. Use Backup & Data, Reset All Data to erase everything the app stores. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data.
