# Profile and Biometrics

## What you can set

| Field | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Display Name | Greeting and sidebar |
| Photo | Avatar. Use **Upload Photo**, **Change** or **Remove** |
| Age | Shown on your profile |
| Weight (kg) | Feeds the [calorie estimate](Calorie-Calculation) |
| Height (cm) | Feeds the BMI rating |
| Proficiency Level | Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced or Elite |
| Primary Fitness Objective | Muscle & Strength, Endurance, Weight Loss, Skill Mastery or General Fitness |
| Daily Calorie Burn Goal | Target shown on the [Dashboard](Dashboard) |
| Daily Movements Goal | Number of exercises you aim to log each day |

Tap **Save Profile & Calorie Parameters** to save. Changes are stored on your phone.

## BMI rating

When height and weight are set, the app shows your Body Mass Index and a range:

| Range | Rating |
| --- | --- |
| Under 18.5 | Under |
| 18.5 to 24.9 | Normal |
| 25 to 29.9 | Over |
| 30 and above | Obese |

BMI is a rough guide. It does not account for muscle mass, so it can be misleading for people who train with weights or bodyweight.
