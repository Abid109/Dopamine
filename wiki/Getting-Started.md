# Getting Started

## 1. Set up your profile

Open **Profile & Bio** from the menu and enter your name, weight and height. Your weight is used to estimate calories, so this makes your numbers more accurate. Set your level, objective and daily goals, then tap **Save Profile & Calorie Parameters**.

## 2. Log your first exercise

1. Open **Log Workout**.
2. Pick a movement from the library, or choose **Custom Exercise** and type a name.
3. Set the sets, reps and minutes.
4. Tap **Log to Today**.

The calories shown are an estimate. See [Calorie Calculation](Calorie-Calculation).

## 3. Watch your progress

The **Dashboard** shows today's sets, calories, your streak and a weekly chart. The streak counts how many days in a row you were active.

## 4. Plan ahead

Open **Calendar Planner**, pick a date and add a routine, either from a template or your own list.

## 5. Keep notes

Use **Notes & Journal** for training cues, ideas and reflections.

## 6. Back up your data

Go to **Settings** and tap **Export JSON** now and then, or share your data to another phone with [Share and Receive](Share-and-Receive).

## Moving around

- Open the menu from the top-left button.
- The Android back button closes menus and popups first, then takes you to the previous page. On the Dashboard, press it twice to exit.
