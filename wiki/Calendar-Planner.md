# Calendar Planner

## Reading the calendar

Each day can show a dot:

| Marker | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Workout Logged | You logged exercises on that day |
| Routine Planned | You scheduled a routine |
| Both Active | Both of the above |

Use the month arrows to move between months, and tap a day to see its details.

## Plan a routine

1. Select a date.
2. Tap **Plan Routine**.
3. Choose a template, or write your own. Templates: **Full Body Blast**, **Push Day Mastery**, **Pull Day Power**, **Legs & Core**.
4. Enter a **Routine Name** and the exercises, one per line.
5. Tap **Save Routine Plan**.

## Manage plans

Planned routines for the selected day are listed under **Planned Routines**. Mark one as completed, or delete it.

## Review history

**Logged Exercises** shows what you did on the selected day, with its calories. **Log Set** takes you to the logger for that date.
